          Manual

          How to use Audit Pro

          A short guide to every screen. Pick a topic — each one explains what you do and what you will see.

          Getting started

          Audit Pro is one file that runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: your audits, templates, answers and photos stay in this browser on this MacBook.

          Your first audit in three steps

          1. Open the app with Live Server (in VS Code: right-click AuditPro.html → Open with Live Server). Do not double-click the file itself — some browsers then refuse to save.
          2. On the Dashboard, click + New audit. Give it a title and date, choose the Audit template (the format of questions), then click Create audit.
          3. The audit opens on Section 1 with the first question already expanded. Answer it and keep going — everything saves automatically.
          What you will see: the menu on the left stays visible on the Dashboard, Audits, Templates and this Manual. Inside an audit it hides so the questions get the full width — the ‹ Audits button at the top left takes you back to the list.

          Good to know: the app works with no internet at all, and your data never leaves this computer.

          The Dashboard

          The Dashboard is the opening screen — a live picture of the whole app. Every tile, chart slice and row on it is a button that takes you somewhere useful.

          The tiles (top row)

          • Audits — how many audits exist. Opens the audits list.
          • Completed / In progress / Not started — click one to show only those audits.
          • Average score — the mean of every audit's score. Click it to see each audit's score, best first.
          • Answered tests and Unanswered — the work done and the work left. Click to list the audits behind the number.
          • Templates, Evidence photos, Corrective actions, Good practices and N/A marked — click to open the matching screen or list.

          The two charts

          • Audit progress — the donut splits your audits into completed, in progress and not started. Click a slice, or a row in the legend, to filter the audit list down to those audits.
          • Answer mix — every enabled test in every audit, split into scored 100%, scored 50%, scored 0%, N/A and unanswered. Click a slice to see which audits make up that part.
          What you will see: clicking a slice or tile opens a detail panel just below the charts. Each row is a button — click it to open that audit, its summary, its corrective actions or its good practices.

          Lower panels

          • Recent audits — your latest audits with a progress bar and score. Click one to resume it, or View all to open the audits list.
          • Templates in use — each saved format, its size and how many audits use it. Manage opens the Templates screen.

          Running an audit

          An audit is one pass through a template's questions. You always see the whole section at once, with one question open.

          The question list

          1. The section is listed as rows: number, question text and a status chip (Not answered, Fail · 0%, Pass · 100%, N/A · not scored).
          2. Click a row to open it. Only one row is open at a time, so the rest of the section stays visible. Click it again to close it.
          3. Choose an answer — Pass, Fail or N/A (the buttons follow the template's answer type). The moment you answer, the comment box and the evidence photos area open underneath.
          4. Open the next question and the previous one closes automatically.

          Moving around

          • Section dropdown — jump to any section; it opens that section's first unanswered question.
          • ‹ › beside the dropdown — previous / next section.
          • Next unanswered — the next question you have not answered yet.

          Keyboard shortcuts

          P Pass · F Fail · N N/A or No · Y Yes · 1–3 answer by position · 0 N/A · Enter next question. Shortcuts apply to the open question.

          What you will see: the bar at the top shows Answered X of Y for the whole audit, and a section count for the section you are in.
          Auto-save: every answer, comment and photo is saved as you go. Closing the app and reopening resumes at the question you were on.

          Templates

          A template is the audit format — the sections and questions you check against. The app ships with one built-in format and you can create as many of your own as you like.

          Editing a template

          1. Open Templates from the menu, then click Edit on a template.
          2. Every section starts collapsed, so you can see the whole format at a glance. Click the › arrow to open a section, or use Expand all / Collapse all.
          3. Use ↑ ↓ on a section header to move the whole section, and ↑ ↓ on a question row to move that question inside its section.
          4. + Add question adds a question to a section; + New section creates a new section.
          5. Disable keeps a question in the format but takes it out of the score; Enable brings it back.
          What you will see: the header says how many tests and sections the template has and how many audits use it. Sections you open stay open while you work.

          Heads up: a template is shared by every audit that uses it, so adding, renaming or disabling tests changes the score of those audits — the score is total test scores ÷ enabled tests. The built-in format's questions can be disabled and reordered, but not renamed or deleted: Duplicate it first to get a fully editable copy.

          Template actions

          • Duplicate — a fully editable copy of a format.
          • Rename — change the template's name and reference.
          • Export — download the template as a JSON file, for backup or to move it to another computer.
          • + New template / Import JSON — start an empty format, or load an exported one back in.
          • Delete — available for templates you made, and only while no audit is using them.

          Reports & Excel

          Every audit can be read on screen, printed to PDF and exported to Excel.

          Audit summary

          1. Inside an audit, click Audit summary (bottom right).
          2. You get the overall score, the formula (total test scores ÷ enabled tests), the counts for 100% / 50% / 0% / N/A / unanswered, and a table for every section.
          3. While the audit is unfinished the score is marked provisional and unanswered enabled tests count as 0%.

          Full report and printing

          1. Click Full report on the summary. It collects the cover page, the score, the section table, every test with its answer, comment and photos, the corrective actions, the good practices and a general comments box you can type into.
          2. Click Print / Save as PDF and choose Save as PDF as the printer for a clean A4 file. Toolbars and buttons are hidden automatically on the printout.

          Excel

          1. Click Export Excel on the summary or on the report.
          2. The workbook downloads as Audit Pro - <title> - <date>.xlsx with four sheets: Summary, Checklist, Corrective Actions and Good Practices.
          What you will see: photos appear in the PDF report. In Excel they are counted per row, not embedded.

          Corrective actions & good practices

          These are two logs that belong to each audit and appear in its report.

          Corrective action plan

          1. Inside an audit click Actions (or Corrective actions on the summary).
          2. Click + Add action and fill in the issue, root cause, corrective action, preventive action, person in charge, due date, priority and status. You can link it to the test it came from.
          3. Add an issue photo, and later an improvement photo to show the fix.

          Good practices

          1. Click Good practices, then + Add good practice.
          2. Record the place or area and what the factory does well, and add a photo if you like.
          What you will see: both lists also appear in the summary counts, the full report and the Excel workbook.

          Photos

          Photos are your evidence. They are stored offline in this browser and are never uploaded.

          1. Click any + Photo button — on an answered question, on a corrective action (issue and improvement photos) or on a good practice.
          2. Choose an image from your computer. It is resized automatically (longest side 1600 px, JPEG) so a phone photo does not fill up storage.
          3. Click the × on a thumbnail to remove it.
          What you will see: thumbnails appear under the question, and the question row shows a photo count chip. The same photos are embedded in the PDF report.

          Backup: photos live in the browser's IndexedDB, separate from the audit text. For a portable copy of the evidence, use the PDF report.

          Data & privacy

          Audit Pro is deliberately offline-only. There is no account, no server and no internet traffic.

          • Audits and templates are stored in this browser's localStorage under the key auditpro.v6.
          • Photos are stored in this browser's IndexedDB.
          • Clearing your browser data for this site removes everything, so keep PDF and Excel exports of audits that matter.
          • Emailing AuditPro.html to someone gives them their own empty copy on their own computer.
          What you will see: the green Saved pill at the top right whenever your latest change has been written to this browser.

          Troubleshooting

          My answers are not being saved

          • Open the app with Live Server rather than double-clicking the file: some browsers block storage for pages opened straight from disk. If saving is blocked the audit shows a yellow warning banner.
          • Check that the top-right pill says Saved and not Not saved.

          The question list is long

          • Only one question is open at a time by design. Use Next unanswered or the section dropdown to move quickly.

          I want different questions

          • Open Templates → Duplicate the built-in format → Edit the copy. Add, rename, disable and reorder freely; the built-in format stays untouched.

          A score looks wrong

          • The score is total test scores ÷ enabled tests. Unanswered enabled tests count as 0%, and N/A removes a test from the score entirely. Editing a template changes the denominator for every audit that uses it.
